# About

Welcome, and thanks for reading. If you want to contact me, the links in the
footer are the best way.

From 2007 to 2014 this blog was named *Litany Against Fear*, after the mantra
from the [Dune novels by Frank Herbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(novel)):

> **I must not fear.**<br>
> **Fear is the mind-killer.**<br>
> **Fear is the little-death that brings total obliteration.**<br>
> **I will face my fear.**<br>
> **I will permit it to pass over me and through me.**<br>
> **And when it has gone past I will turn the inner eye to see its path.**<br>
> **Where the fear has gone there will be nothing.**<br>
> **Only I will remain.**

In 2020 it moved to [quaran.to](https://quaran.to), and in 2026 it moved back
here, with those journal entries in tow.
